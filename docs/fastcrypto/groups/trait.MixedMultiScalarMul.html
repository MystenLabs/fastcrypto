fn mixed_multi_scalar_mul( &self, scalars: &[<Self::Point as GroupElement>::ScalarType], dynamic_points: &[Self::Point], ) -> FastCryptoResult<Self::Point>

Compute the mixed multi-scalar multiplication

a_1*P_1 + ... + a_n*P_n + b_1*Q_1 + ... + b_m*Q_m,

where the P_i are the n fixed points these tables were built for and only their scalars a_i change between calls, while the m points Q_j (= dynamic_points) and their scalars b_j are both given fresh on every call. All n + m scalars are passed as a single slice of the form scalars = [a_1, ..., a_n, b_1, ..., b_m] of length self.num_static_points() + dynamic_points.len(). The a_i*P_i part is evaluated from the precomputed tables, so when the P_i are reused across many calls this is faster than a regular MSM over all n + m points.
